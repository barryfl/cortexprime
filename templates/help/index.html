Cortex Prime System Help

This guide explains the controls and system features provided by the Cortex Prime Foundry module. It is not a replacement for the rules of your Cortex Prime game; your GM decides which Trait Sets, mechanics, and options are in use.

If you are new to the sheet, start with Traits and Trait Sets, then read Dice Pools and Rolling. GMs should also review Actor Configuration.

Quick Sheet Reminders

  • Hover icon-only controls for a tooltip explaining what they do.
  • Changing a die itself changes its permanent value.
  • The small controls beneath a die temporarily step it down, reset it, or step it up.
  • The sheet-level reset control clears all temporary die steps on that Actor.
  • The eye-slash control hides an inherited Trait on only that Actor; it does not delete the template Trait.
  • Adding a Trait to the Dice Pool opens the pool automatically.
  • The Dice Pool is temporary: a successful roll or closing the pool clears it.
