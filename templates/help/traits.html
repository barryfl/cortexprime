Traits and Trait Sets

Trait Set Settings

  • Has Description: Adds a description field to traits in this set.
  • Has Labels: Gives traits both a displayed label and an internal name.
  • Has Descriptors: Allows traits to contain additional descriptive entries.
  • Has SFX: Allows traits to contain SFX entries.
  • Has Sub-Traits: Allows traits to contain nested sub-traits.
  • Has Dice: Gives traits a die rating and enables die controls on the actor sheet.
  • Consumable Dice: Allows dice in the Trait Set to be consumed or removed through normal play.

Important

Settings beneath a disabled parent option may appear greyed out. For example, Consumable Dice cannot be enabled until Has Dice is enabled.

Preset vs Custom Traits

Preset Traits are defined in Actor Settings and are copied into new actors.

Custom Traits are created directly on an individual actor sheet during play when the GM allows custom Traits for that Trait Set. GMs may create them regardless of that player-facing setting.

Permanent vs Temporary Die Values

Changing the die itself edits the Trait's permanent value. The small minus, reset, and plus controls temporarily step its effective die down, restore it to the base value, or step it up without changing that permanent value.

When any Traits are adjusted, the reset control in the sheet tabs clears all temporary Trait steps on that Actor.

Hiding and Restoring Traits

An inherited Trait may be hidden on one Actor without changing the Actor Type or other Actors. Restore it from the Trait Set's Hidden Traits list; its actor-local value and temporary adjustment are preserved.

Resource Traits

Resource Traits track a current numeric value with decrement and increment controls. Depending on GM configuration, an optional image may identify the Resource or act as its consume/decrement button.
